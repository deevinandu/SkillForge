pick_place@0.2.1 · round 1 · parent 0.2.0

pick_place@0.2.1 targets the most common gate failure, grasp_slip (27/100); the ik_failures (23/100) almost all came after a slip, grasp_miss and regrasp. The main change: the ball is carried 3 cm above the place height instead of v0.2.0's 10 cm hover. Slips cluster on low-friction seeds and got worse when I slowed the carry, which suggests the held ball creeps out of the soft finger contacts over time, so a shorter lift and path (the goal region has no walls) loses it far less often. A smaller change: the grip now keeps closing for at least 8 steps and until the measured width stops changing, rather than a fixed 4 steps that was shorter than the up-to-5-step action latency; on its own this took dev from 53% to 60%. Dev success went from 16/30 (53%) with v0.2.0 to 29/30 (97%), and 96/100 on 100 dev seeds; ideas that did not help (smoothed carry, a 90-degree pre-squeeze, averaged observations, a lower grasp height) were removed.

pick_place@0.2.2 · round 2 · parent 0.2.1

pick_place@0.2.2 targets grasp_slip (10/100 gate failures), which in every case ended with a failed re-grasp: the re-grasp descended toward a noisy, low-pass-filtered position and, after a slip, toward a ball that was still rolling (debug logs showed misses 4–18 cm off). It adds a localize step at the pre-grasp hover that waits until a least-squares velocity fit over the last 12 raw observations shows the ball at rest, then descends to their outlier-trimmed mean, switching back to tracking the ball if it moves more than 1.5 cm; it also squeezes for at least 20 steps instead of 8 before lifting, which cut first-attempt slips/misses on 100 dev seeds from 14 to 10. Dev success on 100 seeds went from 96/100 (0.2.1) to 97/100 (2 grasp_slip, 1 ik_failure), and 30-seed dev from 29/30 to 30/30; that gain is within noise, so the gate's paired test should decide. Things I tried and dropped because they measured worse on dev: a speed/acceleration-limited carry (91/100, more ik_failures), changing grasp height (92–95/100, no change in slips), and waiting for the ball to stop before every re-plan plus more miss retries (92–94/100, the waits use up the 300-step budget).

pick_place@0.2.3 · round 3 · parent 0.2.1

pick_place@0.2.2 targets grasp_slip, the most common gate failure of 0.2.1 (10/100). Those slips cluster on low-friction, high-gain or heavy-ball seeds within about 100 steps of the grasp, so the ball is squeezed out by jerky acceleration during lift and transport, not by bad re-grasps. During lift and transport the arm now follows a waypoint that moves at most 2 cm per control step (0.4 m/s) in a straight line toward the goal; the waypoint is reset on every re-grasp attempt, and approach, descent and place are unchanged. A first version limited the target relative to the lagging EE; it stopped the slips but was too slow (70% dev, 7 timeouts), so I replaced it with a waypoint that advances on its own clock. Dev success after the change is 30/30 (100%), and 96/100 on 100 dev seeds (2 slips, 2 ik_failure, 1 collision); I could not re-run 0.2.1 on the dev seeds in this sandbox, so the "before" figure is its gate rate of 85%, not a dev measurement.
