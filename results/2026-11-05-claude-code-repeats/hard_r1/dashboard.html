pick_place@0.2.1 · round 1 · parent 0.2.0

pick_place@0.2.1 mainly targets finger-table collisions and the grasp/release/re-grasp loops in REPORT.md; both came from treating noisy object observations (σ up to 1.5 cm) as exact. The biggest fix is a hold monitor that judges object-to-gripper drift on the mean of the last 8 raw offsets instead of one sample, because single noisy samples kept crossing the 5 cm "lost" threshold, aborting good carries and making the retry open the gripper and drop the cube. Grasp and place heights now come from known geometry (the cube resting on the table, the grasp-time EE height) instead of noisy z, with a minimum TCP clearance above the table; grasp xy uses a median of recent raw observations, and the place xy offset uses a median over the carry. Gripper settle time went from 4 to 10 steps so it exceeds the worst-case action latency (6), and a short xy alignment hold runs above and at the bin before release. Dev success on the 30 dev seeds went from 24/30 (80%, 5 collisions) to 28/30 (93%, 0 collisions), and on 100 dev seeds it is 96/100 (93/100 with a 5-step window); all of this is measured in sim only.
