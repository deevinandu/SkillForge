pick_place@0.2.1 · round 1 · parent 0.2.0

pick_place@0.2.1 targets the dominant failure in REPORT.md: the can physically slipping out of the gripper (`grasp_slip`), after which a botched re-grasp ended in `ik_failure`/`collision`. Diagnostic dev runs (grip-only holds on every seed; slow lift and carry hold; normal speed drops ~1/3) showed the loss is inertial during lift/transport and much worse when the carry direction lies along the finger closing axis. Changes: grasp 3.5 cm below the top (near the centre of mass) instead of 2 cm, pick the cylinder grasp yaw whose closing axis is across the object-to-bin direction, ramp commanded motion while holding (0.25 m/s horizontal, 0.1 m/s vertical, 0.5 m/s² acceleration limit), and compute grasp targets from a median of resting observations instead of the snap-prone filter. Smaller fixes: a TCP height floor so the fingers clear the table on re-grasps, longer grip settle, release 12 mm above the floor, and looser hold-monitor drift thresholds so 12 mm observation noise no longer triggers a false "slip" that opens the gripper. Dev success went from 9/30 (30%) for 0.2.0 to 29/30 (97%) for 0.2.1, and 96/100 on the full 100 dev seeds; all of this is measured in simulation only.
