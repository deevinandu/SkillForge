pick_place@0.2.1 · round 1 · parent 0.2.0

The main cause of failure was the stock hold monitor: it compared single raw observations against a 3 cm drift threshold, and with observation noise around 1.2–1.5 cm per axis it reported false slips while the cube was being carried; the retry then opened the gripper and re-grasped, sometimes from inside the bin, which caused the finger-table collisions and the "still holding" timeouts. v0.2.1 replaces it with a monitor that low-pass filters the object-to-gripper error, plus a hard limit of 8 cm on raw readings so real drops are still caught. Other changes: the grasp is planned from the median of 10 stationary observations, frozen once planned, with the cube's z clamped to its known resting height so the fingers are never aimed into the table. The in-hand offset used for placing and for the monitor is re-measured as a median above the bin, and the placement check uses a median of observations; grip and release also wait until the finger width has actually changed and settled. Dev success went from 24/30 = 80% (v0.2.0) to 29/30 = 97%, and v0.2.1 scored 96/100 on 100 dev seeds (v0.2.0 was not run on 100 seeds); no collisions remain, and the 4 failures left are low-friction slips and one timeout.
