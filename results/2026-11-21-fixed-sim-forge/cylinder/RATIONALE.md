Most gate failures (ik_failure, collision) began with a grasp_slip on low-friction, high-gain, heavy-can episodes. After the slip, the re-grasp from the dropped can ran into ik_failure or finger-table collisions. I slowed the carry ramp: horizontal speed 0.25 to 0.18 m/s, vertical speed 0.1 to 0.07 m/s, and acceleration 0.5 to 0.3 m/s², so inertial loads stay within what the grip can hold. I also set `version` to "0.1.1". Dev success on 30 seeds went from 28/30 (93%) to 30/30 (100%). The gain is within sampling noise at n=30, and the slower carry has not been checked for timeouts on the gate seeds.
