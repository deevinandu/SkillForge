pick_place@0.1.1 · round 1 · parent 0.1.0

The dominant failure was grasp_slip (16/20 gate failures, and all 3 dev failures), and those seeds have large cube yaw (up to 0.785 rad) and often low friction or high mass. v0.1.0 always grasped with a fixed, unrotated top-down gripper, so the fingers closed on the edges or corners of a rotated cube. v0.1.1 turns the gripper to the cube's observed yaw, reduced modulo 90°, so the fingers close on two flat faces. It also smooths the noisy object position, caps how far ahead of the gripper the target is while carrying (gentler lift and transport for heavy, low-friction cubes), and re-grasps up to 3 times if the cube is seen leaving the gripper. Dev success on the standard 30 seeds went from 27/30 (90%, 3× grasp_slip) to 30/30 (100%), and on 100 dev seeds v0.1.1 scores 99/100 (one grasp_slip on seed 100063); v0.1.0 was not run on all 100.
