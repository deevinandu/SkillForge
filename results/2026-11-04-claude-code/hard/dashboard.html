pick_place@0.2.1 · round 1 · parent 0.2.0

The most common gate failure was collision (finger hitting the table), and it almost always came after a first grasp and release that left the cube outside the bin, followed by a re-pick that went too low. To get the cube into the bin on the first try, pick_place@0.2.1 averages the object-to-gripper offset over the whole carry instead of using one noisy reading taken at grip time (observation noise is up to 1.5 cm and the bin leaves about 1 cm of clearance). It also holds grip and release for 10 steps instead of 4, so the gripper command lands before the arm moves, even with up to 6 steps of action latency. To stop the table collisions, the grasp height snaps to the task's nominal resting height and never goes below it, so noisy low z readings no longer drive the fingers into the table; the placement check also now uses the mean of 12 raw observations. Dev success (40 seeds, sim only) went from 31/40 = 78% to 35/40 = 88%, with collisions down from 5 to 0 and wrong_placement from 1 to 0; the remaining failures are 3 timeouts and 2 grasp slips.

pick_place@0.2.2 · round 2 · parent 0.2.1

pick_place@0.2.2 replaces the stock hold monitor with `SmoothedHoldMonitor`, which judges object-to-gripper drift on the mean of the last 8 raw observations (thresholds 4 cm sustained / 7 cm hard) instead of a single raw sample against 3 cm. It targets the dominant gate failures (timeout "still holding" and grasp_slip, 24 of 27): every one of them had observation noise std near 1–1.5 cm, which makes a single sample's 3-D deviation routinely exceed 3 cm, so the monitor declared false slips mid-carry and the slip recovery opened the gripper and re-picked until the 300-step budget ran out. A temporary instrumented run confirmed the extra re-picks were not coming from the post-release placement check, leaving the slip detector as the trigger. Real drops (the cube falling 10 cm or more) still exceed the window-mean thresholds within a few steps, and the width-collapse check is unchanged. Dev success went from 88% (35/40) to 98% (39/40), and 98/100 on 100 dev seeds; the remaining failures are grasp_slip on high-noise or high-yaw seeds.
