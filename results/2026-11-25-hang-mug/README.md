# Hang a mug on a mug stand (sim only)

Everything here is **measured in MuJoCo sim on the Franka Panda model**. Nothing has run on hardware.

## What was added
- Contracts: `ObjectSpec.shape = "mug"` with `size = (outer radius, half-height, wall)` and a
  `MugHandle` (D-shaped loop on the mug's +x side); success kind `object_hung(object, support,
  hold_s=1.0, max_speed=0.15)`.
- Sim (`sim/mug.py`, `sim/success.py`, `sim/mujoco_env.py`): a mug is 20 wall boxes + a bottom disc +
  5 handle capsules (a mesh would collide as its convex hull, filling cup and handle). `object_hung`
  is judged on TRUE state: the peg's axis passes through the handle's clear opening, the mug touches
  only fixed objects (no robot, table, loose object), moves slower than `max_speed`, and all of this
  holds for `hold_s` without a break. A mug impaled on the peg through its cup is not hung (tested).
- Sim fix: the menagerie Panda's fingertip pads stop **3.0 mm apart** when fully closed, so no
  rim pinch on a 3 mm wall was possible. `MujocoEnv(closed_pad_gap=0.0)` moves the pads so they
  meet (0.003 reproduces the old model). All 349 existing tests, including every regression
  baseline, still pass with the change.
- Tasks (`tasks/franka_hang_mug*.json`, generated by `make_tasks.py`): plastic mug 65 mm x 90 mm,
  3 mm wall, 0.2 kg, handle opening 22 x 37 mm; wooden-style stand, 10 mm pegs 70 mm long angled up 30 deg.
  - `franka_hang_mug`: one peg; handle direction uniform over 360 deg per episode; xy jitter 3 cm,
    friction 0.8-1.2x, mass 0.75-1.25x, obs noise up to 2 mm.
  - `franka_hang_mug_handle_fixed`: same, handle always facing the right way (debug / easy rung).
  - `franka_hang_mug_tree`: three pegs at different heights and azimuths, target `peg_front`.
- Seed skill `skills/hang_mug.py` `HangMug` 0.1.0: plans backwards from the hanging pose (mug y axis
  along the peg), rim pinch opposite the handle, wrist yaw chosen to stay far from joint-7 limits,
  targets expressed as mug poses mapped through the mug-in-hand transform, re-measured every step.

## Results (HangMug 0.1.0, sim, Franka)
| task | seeds | success | failures |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| franka_hang_mug (forge gate, null improver) | 0-99 | 82/100 | grasp_slip 14, wrong_placement 3, collision 1 |
| franka_hang_mug (held out) | 5000-5199 | 173/200 (86%) | grasp_slip 21, wrong_placement 3, collision 3 |
| franka_hang_mug_handle_fixed | 0-99 | 90/100 | grasp_slip 9, wrong_placement 1 |
| franka_hang_mug_tree | 0-99 | 82/100 | grasp_slip 10, wrong_placement 5, collision 3 |

Development notes, measured on a few seeds while building:
- Without the pad fix the pinch never holds (fingers close on nothing: width -> 0 around the wall).
- Without re-measuring the mug-in-hand transform, the mug yaws ~20 deg in the pinch while the wrist
  turns and the loop misses the peg (seed 0).
- A threaded mug placed by hand settles in ~1-3 s (condim 4); no `sim_unstable` or ejection events seen.

## One forge round with Claude Code (Pro login via `claude -p`, Sonnet 5.5)
`skillforge forge tasks/franka_hang_mug.json --seed-skill hang_mug --improver claude-code --model claude-sonnet-5-5 --rounds 3 --rollouts 100`
(files in `forge_cc/`). Round 1 changed **one number**, `grasp_depth` 0.018 -> 0.028 m (a deeper
rim pinch), and the paired gate accepted it: 82 -> 99/100 on gate seeds 0-99, +17/-0, p = 7.6e-6.
The run then stopped at the 0.9 target. CLI cost figure $0.21 (Pro: counts against the usage limit, not billed).

The repaired skill (0.1.1) on seeds the agent never saw:
| task | seeds | 0.1.0 | 0.1.1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| franka_hang_mug (held out) | 5000-5199 | 173/200 | 198/200 |
| franka_hang_mug_handle_fixed | 0-99 | 90/100 | 98/100 |
| franka_hang_mug_tree | 0-99 | 82/100 | 99/100 |

All remaining failures are grasp_slip. This is a parameter-only fix that depends on finger and pad
geometry, so by the structural rule it is a robot-specific repair; whether it is also right for
another gripper is untested. The agent's rationale says it tried no other depth. The library seed
in `src/skillforge/skills/hang_mug.py` stays at 0.1.0 so the experiment can be re-run from it.

## Not measured / open
- The claims that a handle grasp or a wrong yaw fails are by construction of the skill, not ablated.
- Perception (`perception/frames.py`) maps one geom per object, so segmentation sees only the
  mug's bottom disc. Not fixed.
